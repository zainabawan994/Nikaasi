# Nikaasi — Brand Identity (Checkpoint 1)

## Name
**Nikaasi** (نکاسی), a twist on *Nikaas*. In Urdu, *nikaasi* is the everyday word for **drainage / outflow**, as in *nikaasi-e-aab* (water drainage). The name says what the system does: it gets water, traffic and people *out* of harm's way.

## Tagline
**"Pani se pehle, plan."** (*Plan before the water.*)
Alternate (English): *Before the street becomes a river.*

## Logo
`public/assets/logo.svg`: a **teal raindrop** with a faint street grid inside it, crossed by a **white outflow arrow**.
- Raindrop: the monsoon, the input signal.
- Street grid: the city, roads and sectors.
- Outflow arrow bending away: *nikaas*, meaning rerouting and draining. The response, not just the report.

Use the logo on Monsoon Night (navy) or white. Keep clear space of ¼ of the logo width around it. Minimum size is 24 px.

| Rain Mist | `#E3F1F4` | Soft backgrounds, explanation boxes |

| Watch | `#8FA3B8` | No confirmed flooding |

| High | `#EE8434` | Confirmed, act |

Severity colours are reused everywhere: map sector tint, badges, citizen status card and alert borders. A colour means the same thing on every screen.

## Typography
- **Space Grotesk** (headings, numbers, clock): technical and confident.
- **Inter** (body/UI): highly legible at small sizes on phones.

## Voice
Calm, specific, bilingual. Every alert goes out in **English and Roman Urdu** and names the road, the action and the emergency number:
> [Nikaasi CRITICAL] Flooding in G-10. Srinagar Hwy — G-10 Underpass closed — use alternate routes. Emergency: 1122.
> [Nikaasi] G-10 mein pani bhar gaya hai. … band hai, doosra rasta lein.
