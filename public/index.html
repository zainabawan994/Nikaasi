How it works

1 · Capture
Citizen reports + PMD rainfall feed + ITP traffic feed (mocked)
2 · Fuse & judge
Weighted fusion + corroboration → severity, confidence, explanation (Gemini + rules)
3 · Plan
Close segments & reroute, dispatch CDA / 1122, alert residents
4 · Simulate
Traffic re-assignment, ticket lifecycle, pumping, SMS delivery
5 · Visualise
Before / after / no-response maps and metrics
