How we use your report

Plain-language summary for people who report flooding through Nikaasi.

What we receive

  • What you write, and the sector you pick or your GPS location (only if you press "GPS").
  • A photo, if you add one.
  • A name, if you type one. It's optional.
  • A random device code stored in your browser. It lets us count your reports once, so repeat reports don't inflate severity.

What we use it for

  • Judging how serious flooding is, together with rainfall and traffic data.
  • Sending drain teams and rescue, diverting traffic, and warning people nearby.
  • Your report is shown to signed-in flood-response staff. Photos are never public.

Google Gemini

When AI analysis is switched on, the text and photo are sent to Google Gemini to understand the report, for example how deep the water looks. Your name and location are not sent. If Gemini is off or slow, the report is read by our own rules instead.

How long we keep it

  • Photos, exact GPS location, name and device code are deleted after 90 days.
  • The report text and sector are kept as a record of the incident.

Your choices

  • You can report without a name, photo or GPS. Picking a sector is enough.
  • Clearing your browser data removes the device code.
  • In an emergency, call Rescue 1122 first.
